01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding Plant Hydraulic Failure

This ongoing research project focuses on understanding cavitation, gas bubble dynamics and embolism formation in biological plant xylem using Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI) modelling techniques.

The study aims to investigate how air bubbles nucleate, grow and interact with fluid flow inside plant vascular systems under different environmental conditions.

RESEARCH MOTIVATION

Why Cavitation Matters

01

Water Transport

Plants transport water through microscopic xylem vessels that form an essential part of the plant vascular system.

02

Cavitation

Under drought or freezing conditions, air bubbles may form inside xylem vessels, leading to cavitation.

03

Embolism

Bubble formation can interrupt water transport and contribute to embolism within the vascular system.
