Bio-Fuel Blend Optimization in SI Engines

Project Type: Personal Engineering Project

Project Overview

This project investigates the performance and emission characteristics of bio-alcohol fuel blends in a single-cylinder Spark Ignition (SI) engine. The objective was to identify an optimal fuel blend that improves engine efficiency while reducing harmful emissions.

Project Motivation

Increasing fuel consumption and environmental concerns have encouraged the development of renewable and cleaner alternative fuels. Bio-alcohols possess oxygenated properties that can improve combustion quality and reduce emissions when blended with conventional gasoline.

This project was undertaken to study the feasibility of bio-alcohol fuel blends as sustainable alternatives for future transportation systems.

Objectives

  • Evaluate the performance of bio-fuel blends in an SI engine.
  • Study combustion behaviour at different operating conditions.
  • Analyze engine efficiency and fuel consumption.
  • Measure emission characteristics.
  • Identify the optimum bio-fuel blend composition.

Experimental Setup

Experimental investigations were conducted on a single-cylinder Spark Ignition engine. Different bio-alcohol fuel blends were prepared and tested under varying compression ratios and operating conditions.

Parameters Investigated

  • Brake Thermal Efficiency (BTE)
  • Brake Specific Fuel Consumption (BSFC)
  • Mechanical Efficiency
  • Volumetric Efficiency
  • Exhaust Gas Temperature
  • Carbon Monoxide (CO)
  • Hydrocarbon (HC)
  • Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)
  • Nitrogen Oxides (NOx)

Key Work Performed

  • Tested 5 bio-alcohol fuel blends.
  • Evaluated performance at 4 compression ratios.
  • Measured 5 performance parameters.
  • Measured 5 emission parameters.
  • Compared blend performance with conventional fuel.

Major Findings

  • Bio-alcohol blends improved combustion characteristics.
  • Certain blends showed higher thermal efficiency.
  • Reduction in harmful exhaust emissions was observed.
  • An optimum blend ratio was identified for balanced performance and emissions.

Skills Applied

  • IC Engine Testing
  • Alternative Fuels Research
  • Performance Analysis
  • Emission Measurement
  • Experimental Data Analysis
  • Engineering Optimization

Future Scope

Future work may involve advanced combustion analysis, CFD-based simulation of bio-fuel combustion, optimization using machine learning techniques, and testing under a wider range of operating conditions.
