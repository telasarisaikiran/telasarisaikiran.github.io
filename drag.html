Drag Reduction of Underwater Vehicles using a Superhydrophobic Coating

Development of a mechanically robust epoxy-based superhydrophobic coating designed for underwater applications, hydrophobicity, corrosion protection and potential drag reduction.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designing a Robust Hydrophobic Surface

This academic project focuses on developing a mechanically robust epoxy-based superhydrophobic coating for underwater applications.

Superhydrophobic surfaces are characterized by very high water contact angles and low sliding angles, resulting from low surface energy and hierarchical micro-nano surface roughness.

The proposed coating combines silicon carbide (SiC) particles, carbon nanotubes (CNTs), epoxy resin and PDMS to improve hydrophobicity, mechanical durability, corrosion resistance and potential underwater drag reduction.

05 / MATHEMATICAL MODEL

Physics-Based Hydrodynamic Analysis

The Basset-Boussinesq-Oseen (BBO) equation is used as the primary physics-based model to describe the motion of a sphere falling through water.

The model considers gravitational, buoyancy, drag and added-mass forces.

Drag coefficient and Reynolds number are used to characterize the hydrodynamic behaviour and evaluate the effect of the superhydrophobic coating on fluid resistance.

BBO MODEL · CONCEPTUAL REPRESENTATION
m dv / dt = Fgravity + Fbuoyancy + Fdrag + Fadded-mass

The model provides a physics-based framework for analysing particle/sphere motion in a fluid and studying hydrodynamic forces.

06 / DRAG ANALYSIS

Comparing Coated vs Uncoated

REFERENCE

Uncoated Surface

Reference surface used for comparison of hydrodynamic behaviour.

MODIFIED SURFACE

Superhydrophobic Coating

Coated surface investigated for changes in hydrodynamic resistance and surface interaction.

Drag reduction is evaluated by comparing the hydrodynamic behaviour of an uncoated reference surface with the superhydrophobic coated surface. The drag reduction efficiency is determined from the difference in drag experienced by the two surfaces.
